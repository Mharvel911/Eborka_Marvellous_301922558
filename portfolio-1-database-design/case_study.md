Crest Tech (simulated fintech)
Organisation: Crest Tech is a fictional Lagos-based fintech created for this portfolio. It is inspired by Nigerian digital payment-collection companies and does not represent any real organisation's systems.
Nature of the business: Crest Tech helps businesses (schools, utilities, shops) collect money from their customers through electronic invoices, multiple payment channels (card, bank transfer, USSD) and connections to several banks. It pays the collected money to each business daily (settlement).
Users of the database: business clients, customer-support staff, the finance team, auditors.
Major data stored: businesses, payers, invoices, payments, banks, payment channels, settlements and an audit log.
Key operations: issue an invoice; process a payment; ignore duplicate bank confirmations; reverse failed payments; calculate daily settlements; report monthly collections.
